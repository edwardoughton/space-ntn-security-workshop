WHY THIS WORKSHOP

Building a research agenda
for secure space networks.

Space networks now support communications, positioning, navigation, timing, and other critical services. This broad wireless attack footprint, combined with high autonomy and limited servicing, creates a range of new security challenges.

Across two days, we will hold a series of industry panels and working sessions to identify urgent research gaps. Information gathered will be used to define a new NSF research strategy for securing space and non-terrestrial networks (NTNs).
